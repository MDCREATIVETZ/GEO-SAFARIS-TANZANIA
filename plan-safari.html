Common questions

Before you enquire

Can the sample safaris be changed?

Yes. They are starting points. Dates, route, activities and accommodation can be adjusted.

How far ahead should I plan?

Planning several months ahead gives you more accommodation choices, especially in busy seasons.

Can you combine safari and Zanzibar?

Yes. A wildlife safari can connect with Zanzibar through scheduled or private flights, depending on your itinerary.

Can families travel privately?

Yes. Private vehicles allow more flexibility for breaks, activities and daily pacing.
